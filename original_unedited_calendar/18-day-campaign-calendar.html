18-DAY CAMPAIGN TO END VIOLENCE AGAINST WOMEN
City Government of Cauayan, Isabela  •  November 25–December 12, 2026
Project S.U.N.S.H.I.N.E. for Safe, Inclusive and Technology-Responsive Communities
COVERAGE All LGU Cauayan Offices and 65 Barangays
CONVERGENCE PARTNERS Province of Isabela, RGADC 2, NAPC, OPAPRU, PNP, DepEd, CSOs, POs, youth groups, and OGP partners
FOCUS AREAS VAWC prevention and response, Technology-Facilitated Gender-Based Violence (TFGBV), cyber trafficking, online sexual exploitation, Safe Spaces, referral systems, survivor support, and community accountability
INTEGRATED PLATFORMS Project S.U.N.S.H.I.N.E., iReport VAWC, Cauayan Safe Line, Cauayan CARES, and GERL/GAD KMS systems
