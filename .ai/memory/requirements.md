# Requirements

## Goal
Produce accurate, high-visibility, print-ready graphic calendar layouts (A3 single-page landscape and A4 two-page portrait) and 300 DPI PNG exports for the City Government of Cauayan, Isabela's **18-Day Campaign to End Violence Against Women and Their Children (Orange Campaign)** running from **November 25 to December 12, 2026** (plus the pre-campaign validation on **November 16 – 20, 2026**).

## Users
- **LGU Cauayan Leadership & GAD Office:** Reviews and approves official campaign schedules, lead offices, partner agencies, and venues.
- **Participating Offices, 65 Barangays, & Convergence Partners:** Uses the printed/digital calendar to coordinate daily activities, venues, and multi-sectoral responsibilities.
- **Public & Community Stakeholders:** Views the published calendar posters across city halls, barangays, and online channels.

## Roles
- **Campaign Lead:** GAD Office & Office of the City Mayor.
- **Daily Activity Leads:** Designated LGU offices and councils per activity block (e.g., PESO/COOP/CEDIPO, City Agriculture & Cooperative Office, City Accounting Office, PDAO & OSCA, CSWDO, Cooperative Office, City Youth Development Office, CHRMO & CODI, MOVE, CDRRMO/Rescue 922/Safe Line, City Health Office, City Legal Office, PNP-WCPD/LCAT-VAWC/BJMP/Justice Sector, Liga ng mga Barangay/VAW Desks, JCD GAD CONNECT, CPDO/GAD KMS Team, City Tourism/Culture Office/PIO).
- **Convergence Partners:** National government agencies (DILG, PNP, BJMP, BFP, DepEd, DOLE, OWWA, DTI, DA, COA, PAO, NAPC, OPAPRU, PCW), provincial/regional bodies (Province of Isabela, RGADC 2), barangays, CSOs, POs, OGP partners, faith-based groups, youth groups, academe, JCI, ISAC, and private/tech partners.

## Non-functional requirements
- **Print Resolution & Fidelity:** 300 DPI pixel dimensions (`4961x3508` for A3, `2480x3508` for A4) with `-webkit-print-color-adjust: exact; print-color-adjust: exact;`.
- **Zero Content Clipping:** All text, badges, partner boxes, and venue boxes must fit within fixed-height flex/grid cards without vertical or horizontal overflow.
- **Visual Hierarchy & Legibility:** Distinct color-coded visual blocks for Lead Offices (warm orange tint), Activities (white/cream surface with orange left accent on multi-activity items), Partners (fuchsia/purple tint), and Venues (emerald green tint).

## Security
- Static local HTML/CSS/PNG files with no user input forms, authentication, or sensitive personal data.

## Audit
- Schedule revisions are tracked in Git commit history and verified via `scratch/extract_calendar_text.py`.

## Reporting/export
- High-resolution PNG export via `scratch/render_a3_png.py` and `scratch/render_a4_png.py`.
- Plain-text schedule extraction via `scratch/extract_calendar_text.py`.

## Out of scope
- Interactive web application features, dynamic backend databases, user login/authentication, or mobile responsive reflows (layouts are fixed-dimension print canvases).
