# User Roles

| Role | Purpose | Permissions |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign Organizer (LGU Cauayan / GAD Office / Office of the City Mayor) | Defines official 18-Day Orange Campaign activities, dates, lead offices, partners, and venues. | Approves schedule revisions and final print deliverables. |
| Activity Lead Office | Leads execution of assigned daily campaign activities (e.g., GAD Office, PESO/COOP/CEDIPO, CSWDO, PDAO & OSCA, CHRMO & CODI, CDRRMO, City Health, City Legal, PNP-WCPD, Liga ng mga Barangay, CPDO, City Tourism/PIO). | Coordinates with assigned partner agencies and venues as listed on each day card. |
| Convergence Partner | Supports daily activities across the 65 Barangays, city offices, schools, and community sectors. | Participates in scheduled events and online advocacy tracks. |
| Repository Maintainer / Designer | Updates HTML/CSS calendar files and executes headless Chrome rendering scripts (`scratch/render_a3_png.py`, `scratch/render_a4_png.py`). | Full local read/write access to root HTML/PNG files and `scratch/` scripts (must not modify `do not touch/`, `keep copy/`, or `original_unedited_calendar/`). |
